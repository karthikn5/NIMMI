Adds your Nimmi AI assistant to every page of your WordPress site.

== Description ==

Your Nimmi assistant answers visitors from your business knowledge, in their language, collects their name, phone and email, and hands the conversation to your team when needed.

This copy of the plugin is already connected to your assistant ({{BOT_NAME}}). Upload it, activate it, and the chat button appears on every page.

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress, go to Plugins, Add New, Upload Plugin.
2. Choose the .zip file you downloaded from Nimmi and select Install Now.
3. Select Activate. The chat button appears on your site right away.
4. Optional: Settings, Nimmi AI Chat lets you turn the button off or show a different assistant.
